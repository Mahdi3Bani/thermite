# Code of conduct

Thermite adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), as its code of conduct. The Covenant's own text is what applies. The summary below is only a quick guide to it.

It applies in every space of the project: the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, Thermite's Discord server, and wherever you speak for the project in public.

## In short

- **Be kind and welcoming.** Respect people whose experience, views and backgrounds differ from yours.
- **Criticize work, not people.** Give and take feedback on changes with care. When you make a mistake, own it and learn from it.
- **No harassment.** No insults, no personal or political attacks, no sexual language or attention, and no unwelcome contact.
- **Respect privacy.** Never publish someone's private information, such as where they live or how to contact them, without their permission.
- **Think of the community.** Do what is best for the project and for the people in it, not only for yourself.

## Report a problem

Report a problem with someone's conduct privately to the maintainers, through GitHub's private reporting form for this repository:

1. Open the repository's [**Security** tab](https://github.com/iammrduncan/thermite/security), then **Report a vulnerability**. Or go straight to the [form](https://github.com/iammrduncan/thermite/security/advisories/new).
2. Start the title with **Conduct report:**, so that the maintainers know it is not a security problem.
3. In the description, say what happened, where and when, and who was involved. Add links or screenshots if you have them.

Only the maintainers see the report. Don't report conduct in a public issue, a pull request or a public Discord channel.

## What happens next

The maintainers read every report, and keep the name of the person who reported it private. They look at what happened, and answer in the report. When someone broke the code of conduct, the maintainers act as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines say: from a private correction, to a warning, to a ban for a time, to a ban for good. They may also remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break it.
